In short

formatos-layout is a catalog of video formats for people who record with a presenter (avatar) and show content alongside: a map, a chart, a screenshot or a photo. Each format has a fixed name, such as Largo (Wide), Bolha (Bubble) or Telejornal (Newscast). That way you can ask for "build it in Bolha 9:16" without explaining the layout again. It ships a script that builds the final video from two files, the content video and the presenter video. To use it, all you need is ffmpeg and Python installed.

What it is

A name for every way to build a video

Until now each project had its own layout hidden in the code. Now the formats live in one place, with a name, an example and a command.

🏷️ Fixed name

Largo (Wide), Canto (Corner), Bolha (Bubble), Telejornal (Newscast), Dividido (Split), Apresentador (Presenter), Documentário (Documentary), Aula (Lesson), Faixa (Strip), Viral, Metade (Half), Invertido (Inverted), Pergunta (Question), Empilhado (Stacked) and Personagem (Character). The name becomes the request.

🎞️ Real example

Each format has a 10 to 16 second clip built from a real video, each one with a different event from the Eventos Extremos shorts (Itaipu, typhoon, El Niño, hail, wildfires, storm alert in southern Brazil).

⚙️ One command

compor.sh builds any format with ffmpeg alone, no GPU and no need to record the avatar again.

16:9 · horizontal

The 8 horizontal formats

For YouTube, lessons and long explainers. Click to watch the example.

Largo · Wide largo

Content on the left two thirds and the presenter in a column on the right, with an amber divider.

Explainer with lots of charts; the face always visible.

Canto · Corner canto

Full-screen content and the face in an amber frame in the bottom corner.

When the visual is the star (3D map, screen demo).

Bolha · Bubble bolha

Full-screen content and the face in a circle with an amber ring.

Same as Canto, with a lighter look.

Telejornal · Newscast telejornal

Full-screen content; the presenter takes the whole screen in the chosen segments.

Eye-to-eye opening and closing, full content in the middle.

Dividido · Split dividido

Half content, half presenter, side by side.

Conversation, comparison, equal weight between speech and content.

Apresentador · Presenter apresentador

The presenter dominates the screen and the content sits in a card on the left.

Opinion and personal message; the content is just support.

Documentário · Documentary documentario

Only the content with the voice over it; the presenter appears in the last seconds.

Narrative; the face only at the close (CTA).

Aula · Lesson aula

Content full screen and a small face in an amber frame in the top-right corner.

Lessons and calm explainers; the face doesn't compete with the content. From explicavideos.

There is no Viral in 16:9. Under the INEMA design kit rule, the viral style is for vertical video only.

9:16 · vertical

The 12 vertical formats

For reels, shorts and TikTok.

Faixa · Strip faixa

Content on top and the presenter in a strip below.

Explainer reel, face the whole time.

Canto · Corner canto

Full-screen content and the face in an amber frame at the bottom right.

Reel where the visual leads.

Bolha · Bubble bolha

Full-screen content and the face in a circle with an amber ring.

Same as Canto, lighter.

Telejornal · Newscast telejornal

Full-screen content; the presenter takes over the screen in the chosen segments.

Hook and CTA with the face.

Apresentador · Presenter apresentador

The presenter large at the bottom and the content in a square card on top.

Personal commentary on a data point.

Viral · Viral viral

Hard cuts every 2 s between content, presenter and close-up. Ends on the presenter.

High retention. The full viral style (stamps, captions) is the viral profile of explicavideos.

Documentário · Documentary documentario

Only the content with the voice; the presenter appears in the last seconds.

Narrated reel, face only at the CTA.

Metade · Half metade

Content on top and a large avatar in the bottom half.

Viral reel where the talk weighs as much as the content. From explicavideos viral.

Invertido · Inverted invertido

Avatar on top and the content below.

When the face opens the conversation and the content answers. From r7e.

Pergunta · Question pergunta

Large strip on the hook, then Corner; on the question the avatar grows in the middle of the screen.

A radar or news piece that ends with a question to the audience. From finviral.

Empilhado · Stacked empilhado

Three bands: content on top, avatar in the middle and more content below.

Promo with a headline on top and text or video below. From promoavatar.

Personagem · Character personagem

The character fills the whole video, with no separate content.

UGC, testimonial, an AI character talking straight to camera. From ugc-agente.

How it works

Two videos in, one out

The content and the presenter are recorded separately. The format only decides how the two appear together.

Content video+ Presenter video→ compor.sh --formato→ Final video with voice at −14 LUFS
Prerequisites

What you need

No GPU and no paid service. Runs on Linux or macOS.

ffmpeg

Does all the compositing.

# Ubuntu/Debian
sudo apt install ffmpeg

Python 3 + Pillow

Handles the timing math and the Bolha mask.

pip install pillow

Two videos

The content already in its final aspect (1920×1080 or 1080×1920) and the presenter in 16:9 with the voice.

# e.g.: avatar from the HeyGen studio
cena.mp4  avatar.mp4
User guide · step by step

Building a video in a format

The commands below are the same ones that generated the examples on this page.

1

Download the project

One script and a support folder.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/formatos-layout
cd formatos-layout
2

Pick the format by name

See the list above or FORMATOS.md (in Portuguese). The name goes in lowercase and without accents.

# 16x9: largo canto bolha telejornal dividido apresentador documentario aula
# 9x16: faixa canto bolha telejornal apresentador viral documentario metade invertido pergunta empilhado personagem
./compor.sh --help
3

Build the video

Pass the format, the aspect, the two videos and the output file.

./compor.sh --formato bolha --aspecto 16x9 \
  --cena cena.mp4 --avatar avatar.mp4 --saida video-bolha.mp4
4

Adjust the segments (Telejornal and Documentário)

In Telejornal you choose when the presenter takes over the screen. In Documentário, how many seconds they appear at the end.

./compor.sh --formato telejornal --aspecto 9x16 --cena c.mp4 --avatar a.mp4 \
  --saida tj.mp4 --trechos "0-3,25-28,40-46"
./compor.sh --formato documentario --aspecto 16x9 --cena c.mp4 --avatar a.mp4 \
  --saida doc.mp4 --fim 8
5

Extras: clip, soundtrack, name and face

--ss/--t cut out a clip; MUSICA adds a low soundtrack that ducks under the voice; --nome changes the name tag; AV_CX/AV_CY tell where the face is in the presenter video.

MUSICA=trilha.mp3 AV_CX=1110 ./compor.sh --formato canto --aspecto 9x16 \
  --cena c.mp4 --avatar a.mp4 --saida reel.mp4 --ss 8 --t 10 --nome "ANA | INEMA"
